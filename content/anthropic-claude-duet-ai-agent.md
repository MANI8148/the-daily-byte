Anthropic launches Claude Duet, an AI agent that aims to rival Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots. The new tool arrives as a research preview and is ready for professionals and small business owners to test on iPhone, Android, or via the web. By offering a standalone app and a web version, Anthropic hopes to make the agent widely accessible without requiring a paid Claude subscription.

## What is Claude Duet?

Claude Duet is Anthropic’s answer to the growing demand for autonomous AI helpers that can act on behalf of users. The agent runs on top of Claude 3, the company’s latest large language model, and is packaged as a conversational assistant that can interpret prompts and execute simple workflows. Unlike a pure chatbot, Duet is positioned to handle recurring tasks such as drafting messages, summarizing documents, and organizing schedules, according to the company’s announcement. The interface mirrors familiar messaging apps, letting users type or speak requests and receive instant responses.

## How Claude Duet differs from Muse and Dots

While Meta’s Muse focuses on creative content generation and OpenAI’s Dots targets coding assistants, Claude Duet is built for general professional productivity. Anthropic emphasizes a balanced approach that blends text, list, and calendar functions, aiming to integrate seamlessly into existing workflows rather than replace them. The agent also offers a research preview model that can be toggled on or off, giving users control over how much autonomy the AI receives, a flexibility not highlighted in the Muse or Dots launches. Early testers report that the agent can cut meeting preparation time by roughly half, though formal metrics have not been released.

## Availability and access

Claude Duet launches today as a free research preview. Users can download the app from the Apple App Store or Google Play, or simply visit claude.ai/duet on any browser. The web version mirrors the mobile experience, allowing access from laptops and desktops without installation. The article notes that the preview will run for an initial 30‑day period, after which feedback will shape the final product, but no pricing details have been disclosed. Early adopters have already shared screenshots of the interface on social media, indicating strong interest despite the limited rollout. The company plans to gather usage data to improve safety and reliability. You can try the app now with a single command:

```bash
open https://claude.ai/duet
```

## Target users: professionals and small business owners

The agent is explicitly marketed toward professionals who need help with daily admin work and small business owners who must juggle multiple tasks with limited staff. By automating routine steps such as drafting client emails, summarizing market reports, or scheduling appointments, Claude Duet aims to free up time for higher‑value activities. The announcement cites case studies where a freelance consultant reduced email drafting time by about 40 % and a boutique retailer cut inventory reporting time in half, illustrating the potential efficiency gains. These examples suggest that even modest automation can produce measurable time savings for small teams. The tool’s early feedback loop will help refine these estimates. If the preview proves stable, broader enterprise adoption could follow, expanding the agent’s reach beyond individual freelancers.

## Research preview details and limitations

Anthropic frames Claude Duet as a research preview, meaning the system is still being refined and may produce inconsistent results. The company warns that the agent should not be relied upon for critical decisions without human verification, a caution echoed in the article’s discussion of safety protocols. Since the preview is free, users can experiment without financial risk, but the limited runtime of 30 days means that long‑term workflow integration must be tested quickly. The article also mentions that Anthropic plans to collect usage metrics to improve the model’s reliability and reduce hallucination rates, which are common concerns with large language models. Early testers report occasional delays in response time, but the team says performance is improving each week.

## Potential impact on workflow and productivity

If widely adopted, Claude Duet could reshape how professionals allocate their time, turning repetitive admin work into quick AI‑driven actions. The ability to generate drafts, summarize data, and schedule meetings in seconds may boost productivity by up to 20 % in early adopter reports, though rigorous studies are still pending. For small businesses, the agent offers a low‑cost way to automate customer communication and inventory updates, potentially lowering operational overhead without hiring additional staff. The tool’s integration with existing calendar and email platforms means users can embed AI assistance directly into their daily routines, reducing context switching and improving focus.
