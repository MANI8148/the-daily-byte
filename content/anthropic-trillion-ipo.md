Anthropic has filed to go public, targeting a valuation of up to $2 trillion after reporting $16.3 billion in revenue for the first half of 2026.

## Revenue Shows Strong Growth
Anthropic’s interim 2026 revenue rose 45% year‑over‑year, reaching $16.3 billion. The increase stems from expanded enterprise contracts and higher usage of its cloud‑based AI APIs.

## IPO Filing Details
The company submitted a Form S‑1 to the SEC, outlining a potential share price range of $30‑$35 and a target market capitalization of $2 trillion. The filing also discloses a plan to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker “ANTH.”

## Target Valuation and Market Position
A $2 trillion valuation would place Anthropic among the world’s most valuable technology firms, exceeding the current market caps of Nvidia and Microsoft. The valuation implies a revenue multiple of roughly 123 times the $16.3 billion reported.

## AI Safety Warnings
The prospectus warns that existing safety measures may be insufficient to prevent catastrophic risks, urging regulators to consider stricter oversight of frontier AI development.

## Competitors Pursue Public Listings
OpenAI and AI chipmaker Cerebras have each filed for IPOs, signaling a broader industry trend of AI companies seeking public‑market capital to fund rapid scaling.

## What This Means for Developers
Developers can expect tighter API pricing, faster rollout of safety features, and heightened hiring demand as Anthropic scales. Monitoring the company’s public disclosures will help anticipate product changes.
